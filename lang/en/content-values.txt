What is Personal Accident Insurance?
Accidents can happen at any time, whether you are driving, traveling, working, exercising, or going about your daily life. Personal Accident (PA) insurance helps protect you financially if an unexpected accident causes injury, disability, or death.
Coverage and benefits depend on the plan you choose. They may include medical expenses resulting from an accident, permanent disability, loss of organs or bodily functions, and accidental death.
Unlike general health insurance, which focuses on illness and disease, Personal Accident insurance specifically covers injuries and losses caused by accidents.

Purpose of Personal Accident Insurance
This Personal Accident policy covers accident-related risks, including medical expenses. It offers affordable premiums relative to the coverage provided and is suitable for individuals aged 16 to 60, subject to underwriting conditions. It can help reduce financial burdens, especially for frequent travelers, people in higher-risk occupations, and primary household earners.

Underwriting Conditions
Each insured person may hold a maximum of one policy under this program.
Applicants aged 1 to 75 years old are eligible. Age is determined on the policy effective date.
Coverage begins two days after successful premium payment.
Only applicants in occupation classes 1 and 2, as classified by the Company, are eligible.
The insured must not have a permanent disability before the policy effective date.
This is an annual Personal Accident insurance policy.
Medical expenses must be incurred within 52 weeks from the date of the accident.
If the insured has government benefits, other benefits, or other insurance, the Company will cover only the shortfall.
Inpatient room benefits do not include private nurse fees, braces, wheelchairs, external prostheses, alternative medicine, or acupuncture.
Fracture benefits are limited to a maximum of 100% of the sum insured and must be confirmed by X-ray.
An inpatient stay must last at least six consecutive hours, unless the insured dies during treatment.
Two or more treatments for the same cause within the period specified by the Company will be considered one treatment.
Policy Exclusions
An act committed while under the influence of alcohol, intoxicating substances, or narcotic drugs.
Suicide, attempted suicide, or intentional self-injury.
Infection, disease, parasites, or treatment unrelated to a covered accident.
Miscarriage, food poisoning, and dental treatment, except when treatment is required as a result of an accident.
Back pain caused by disease or degeneration of the spine.
War, riot, terrorism, nuclear radiation, or radioactivity.
Car racing, hazardous sports, boxing, parachuting, bungee jumping, and scuba diving with an air tank.
Riding or traveling by motorcycle unless the coverage has been extended.
Travel on an aircraft not registered to carry passengers or not operated by a commercial airline.
Fighting, committing a crime, being arrested, or evading arrest.
Military, police, or volunteer duties during war or suppression operations.
Osteoporosis, pathological fractures, or fractures caused by a congenital disease.
Injury or illness that existed before applying for insurance.
Occupations Not Covered
Motorcycle taxi drivers, general laborers, construction workers, automotive mechanics, mine workers, fishermen and commercial fishing workers, window cleaners, including high-rise window cleaners, electricians, high-voltage electricians, surveyors, industrial machinery mechanics, security guards, firefighters, stunt performers, passenger transport drivers, taxi, public transport, and truck drivers, professional racing drivers, professional boxers, professional divers, extreme sports athletes, professional athletes, mountaineers, mine drilling and operations workers, oil or natural gas rig workers, boat operators, rescue officers, volunteer rescue workers, antenna or billboard installers, technical or vocational students, couriers and document delivery workers, collection or delivery workers, industrial factory or gas production workers, laborers or other occupations with an equivalent risk level, police officers or military personnel, pilots and flight crew.
